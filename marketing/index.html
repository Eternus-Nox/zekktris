What's new

The latest additions, free like everything else.

New

Zekk Pet

Keep a DM streak going for three days and you can adopt a pet together. You both see the same egg, and small daily tasks like saying hi, chatting and poking each other raise it from Baby to Legendary.

New

Disappearing messages

Set a DM's messages to vanish for both of you once they've been read: after 30 seconds, a day, four weeks or a time you choose. A stopwatch marks each one.

New

Seven new achievement families

Regular, Crowd Pleaser, The People Have Spoken, Kindred Spirits, Now You See Me, Zekk Keeper and Perfect Day join the badges you can earn and show on your profile.

New

Free for everyone

Nothing costs money. Themes, avatar frames and name tags are free on every account, sharper screen sharing comes as a free perk, and there are no ads.

Everything included

Everything built in. Nothing held back.

Voice, video, real privacy and the controls families need, on every account. No "boost to unlock", no paid tier, no ads.

Communities, your way

Servers with text, voice, stage, forum and link channels, threads, events with RSVPs, your own emoji and stickers, and Discovery to help new people find you.

Roles & moderation

34 permissions with per-channel overrides and reaction roles, plus AutoMod filters for words, links, mention spam and repeated messages, timeouts, member reports and an exportable audit log.

Bots & webhooks

Add bots with slash commands, and pipe in alerts from GitHub Actions, Uptime Kuma or anything that posts Discord- or Slack-style webhooks.

Voice, video & stage

Drop into voice channels with noise suppression and push-to-talk, call any DM or group DM, and host stage talks where the audience raises a hand to speak.

Screen share up to 4K

Share a screen or window from desktop, a browser or Android at 720p for everyone. Free perks unlock 1080p at 60 fps, 2K or 4K, handed out by whoever runs your Zekktris server: the Zekktris team on zekktris.com, or you if you host your own.

Rich messaging

Markdown, replies, forwards, reactions, polls, voice messages, scheduled messages, pins, saved messages and search by person or date.

Encrypted DMs & calls

Switch on end-to-end encryption in a one-on-one DM with a password you both know, and its messages, files and calls are locked so not even the server can read them.

Private by default

Only friends can message or call you unless you say otherwise, link previews never show your IP to other sites, and passkeys, two-factor and app lock guard your account.

Parental supervision

Sign-up is 13+, and teens can link to a parent who can limit DMs, calls and servers, approve friend and server requests and set watch-words, until supervision ends at 18.

Streaks, pokes & achievements

Keep a daily DM streak with a heads-up before it breaks, send pokes from a chat, a widget or your Apple Watch, and earn badges to show on your profile.

Make it yours

Twelve themes (six colours, each with a pure-black OLED twin) that recolour the app icon on iPhone and desktop, and now your notifications too: unread badges, in-app banners and Android's alerts. Plus avatar frames, banners, name tags and custom statuses.

Everywhere you are

iPhone and iPad (with widgets and Siri), Apple Watch, Android, Windows, macOS, Linux and any browser, and your status follows the device you're actually using.

Run it yourself

Run your own Zekktris server

Zekktris runs on zekktris.com, and anyone can run their own server too: free, and set up with one command on a machine you control, like a spare PC, a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, a NAS or a small VPS.

  • Your server, your data. Accounts, messages and uploads live on your machine, and you decide who can sign up.
  • One command sets it up. ./install.sh checks Docker, your network, firewall and router (NAT, carrier-grade NAT and UPnP included), writes fresh secrets, builds everything and creates your admin account.
  • Reach it your way. By your own domain with HTTPS, by just your public IP with a free Let's Encrypt certificate, or only on your home network.
  • The apps connect to it. The iPhone and Android apps switch servers from their sign-in screen (the desktop app too, from its next update), and the web app is served straight from your server.
  • Calls included. Voice, video and screen sharing run on a voice server that comes with the install. No outside accounts.
  • Safe updates and backups. Updates take a backup first and roll back by themselves if the new version fails its health check. Backups, restores and health checks are one command each.

Or add it to your home screen

Prefer not to install anything? Zekktris also works as a home-screen web app. Open it in your browser, then:

iPhone & iPad — Safari
  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an up-arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android — Chrome
  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).

The installed web app runs full-screen with its own icon, and the app inside follows the theme you pick in Settings.
