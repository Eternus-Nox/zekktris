Install it on your phone

Zekktris installs as a home-screen app on mobile — no app store needed. Open it in your browser, then:

iPhone & iPad — Safari
  1. Open the app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon (square with an up-arrow).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Android — Chrome
  1. Open the app in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu (top-right).
  3. Choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).

The installed app runs full-screen with its own icon — and the icon & theme follow whatever you pick in Settings.
